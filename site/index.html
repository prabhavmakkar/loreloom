Your book stays yours

Loreloom is software you install, not a website you upload to. That changes everything about who owns your work.

  • It lives on your laptopYour book is a folder on your own computer. We have no servers, so there is nothing of yours for us to see, sell, or lose.
  • No account, no trackingYou don't sign up for Loreloom. It doesn't phone home or count what you do.
  • Plain files you can open anywhereEvery chapter is a simple text file. If you stop using Loreloom tomorrow, your book opens in any other app.
  • Works offlineWrite on a plane or in a cabin. Only the AI helper needs the internet.

What it costs

Loreloom is free and always will be. The AI helper is optional, and it runs on a Claude plan you pay Anthropic for directly, so there's no extra AI subscription layered on top.

Install Loreloom

It takes about three minutes. You'll paste one line into your computer's command window, and the installer does the rest. No admin password needed.

  1. Open Terminal

    Press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, and press Return. A small window with text opens.

  2. Paste this line and press Return

    curl -fsSL https://getloreloom.vercel.app/install.sh | bash

    Paste with ⌘ V. You'll see the installer's progress as it works.

  3. Answer two quick questions

    It asks whether to set up the AI helper and whether to sign in to Claude. Say no if you just want to write; you can add it later.

  4. Start writing

    Loreloom opens in your browser. Next time, open it like any other app: press ⌘ Space and type Loreloom.

What does that line do?

It downloads the installer and runs it. Everything goes into your own user folder:

  • A private copy of Node.js, the engine Loreloom runs on, checked against its official checksum. If you already have it, it's reused.
  • Loreloom itself, plus a Loreloom app icon.
  • If you say yes, Claude Code, using Anthropic's own official installer.

You can read the Mac and Linux installer or the Windows one before running them. Running the line again updates Loreloom and never touches your books.

Questions

Do I need to know anything about computers?

No. If you can copy and paste, you can install Loreloom. After that it's an app you open like any other.

Why does it open in my web browser?

The browser is just the window. Loreloom runs on your computer, and the page you see is served from your own laptop, not the internet. You can keep writing with Wi-Fi switched off.

Where is my book saved?

In a folder called Loreloom in your home folder. Each chapter is a plain text file, so you can back it up, put it in Dropbox, or open it in any editor.

Can I use it without the AI?

Yes. Everything except the helper panel works without it, for free, forever.

Will the AI write my book for me?

Only if you ask, and only where you say. It brainstorms, drafts passages you can take or leave, and reviews your writing like a thoughtful reader. Nothing goes into your chapter until you click to insert it.

Does the AI send my book anywhere?

When you use the helper, the parts of your book it needs are sent to Claude through your own Claude account, the same way as when you chat with Claude yourself. Loreloom itself never sees your login or sends your book anywhere else.

How do I update or uninstall it?

To update, run the install line again. To uninstall on a Mac, delete Loreloom from your Applications folder and the hidden .loreloom folder in your home folder. Your books in the Loreloom folder stay put either way.

Who makes it, and how is it free?

Loreloom was started by a writer who got stuck on a fantasy novel and wanted better tools. It's open source, so anyone can read the code, improve it, or keep it alive. There are no servers to pay for, because everything runs on your laptop.
