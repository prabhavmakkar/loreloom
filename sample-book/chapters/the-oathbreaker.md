The throne room was built of black glass, and every surface held a smaller, hungrier copy of the fire.

"Kneel," said the [Queen](@character/queen). She did not raise her voice. Rooms like this one did that work for her.

[Kael](@character/kael) did not kneel. He stood with Mira's iron nail closed in his fist and felt the court's magic recoil from it, a ripple through the glass like a flinch. "My father swore you an oath, Majesty. He did not swear it on my behalf."

"Aldric swore on his blood, and you are his blood." The Queen smiled. "Oathbreaker is a name you inherit, boy. Ask anyone in the kingdom."

Behind him, quietly, [Mira](@character/mira) took a step toward the door and did not look back at either of them.
