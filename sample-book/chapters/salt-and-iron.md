They walked the salt road for two days, where the ground cracked white and the wind tasted of old seas.

[Mira](@character/mira) kept a hand on her satchel of iron nails. "Ember-magic hates iron," she said, the way another woman might say the sky hates rain. "Press it to a burning thing and the fire forgets itself. That's the only reason we crossed the [Ember Gate](@place/ember-gate) alive."

"And the [Queen](@character/queen)?" [Kael](@character/kael) asked. "Does her fire forget itself too?"

Mira did not answer at once. Far behind them, a column of smoke stood against the sky, thin and patient. "She has hunters on this road. Ashbound men, who have given her their shadows. You should not be able to feel them, but you do, don't you?"

He did. It sat in his chest like a swallowed coal. His father, Aldric, had felt it too, in the last year, before the oath consumed him.
