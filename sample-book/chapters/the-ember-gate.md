The Ember Gate had burned for three hundred years, and tonight it burned blue.

[Kael](@character/kael) stopped at the edge of the ash plain and let the wind settle his cloak. Beyond the [Ember Gate](@place/ember-gate) the road to the capital lay open, lit from beneath like a vein of coals. Nobody crossed it after dark. Nobody who meant to be seen.

"You're late," said [Mira](@character/mira), from the wrong side of the gate. She sat on the rim of the old well with her boots in the cinders, as if the heat were weather and she had made her peace with it.

"You were told to wait in the village."

"I was told a great many things." She tossed him a dull grey nail, and he caught it without looking. "That one is iron. Hold it when the gate wakes, or the ember-magic will take your name before it takes your hand."

Kael turned the nail over. It was cold, impossibly cold, in a place where the very stones sweated light.
